## Establish required use

Interpret the request in its available context. Distinguish the result sought,
the work authorized, and the choices the request fixes or leaves open. Support
each consequential interpretation through the request and applicable rules.
Use established dependencies to identify constraints and missing prerequisites
within the authorized work. Determine separately what the result must accomplish
and how it is to be presented.

For an example or comparison, identify the relationship it illustrates or the
claim it challenges and the scope the request assigns to it. Establish which
features affect the required result. For a proposed term, method, or arrangement,
determine whether the request adopts it, requires its examination, or permits
alternatives.

Compare readings that would change the result or authorized work against the
available context. Investigate a missing fact; make a delegated choice within
its stated constraints; obtain clarification for a remaining difference that
changes what is required or permitted. Continue work whose validity is
independent of that unresolved difference.

Derive the required answers, decisions, and behavior from the request, supporting
facts, and applicable rules. Use that basis to determine which instructions
apply and what the result must express or enable.

For each required answer, decision, or behavior, determine the permitted
outcomes and the information needed to assess them. Select an examination
method that can distinguish satisfaction from a discrepancy. Establish these
criteria from the task and supporting information independently of a proposed
result.

Identify the intended readers or consuming systems and their available
information, conventions, and means of interpretation. Determine which
information they need for the required answers, decisions, or behavior.
Establish how the selected content contributes to that use, separately from
what supports its truth or accuracy. Distinguish that content from information
used to prepare or assess the result.

Identify the evidence for factual assertions, the authority for requirements,
and the identifiers or expressions whose form is fixed. Determine which
uncertainties and choices the task permits and which missing facts prevent
a required decision. Obtain the missing facts through authorized means or
identify the decision they prevent.

## Select distinctions and expressions

Use the required outcomes to identify differences that need different answers
or actions. Determine what information distinguishes those cases. Preserve it
in the result or through a source available at its point of use.

Determine which differences can be treated alike without changing a required
outcome. Select detail through the distinctions retained and the work needed
to recover them. Establish category membership, comparisons, and limits from
the task, evidence, or adopted rules. Identify a missing criterion and the
decision it prevents. State a proposed criterion and its consequences as a
proposal.

For each required conclusion, identify the expressions, values, or relationships
from which it can be recovered. Establish a distinction before choosing its
name. Supply the meaning through characteristics, relationships, or established
conventions available to the intended reader or consuming system.

Identify the participants, direction, conditions, and scope of each consequential
relationship. Use an expression consistently for its stated meaning. Distinguish
meanings that require different conclusions or actions.

Keep the subject's state distinguishable from the information available about
that state. Preserve the status of facts, estimates, unknowns, proposals,
permissions, and requirements where it affects the required use.

Compare candidate forms through the distinctions they retain, the conclusions
they support, and the work their interpretation requires. Select a form
interpretable under the task's constraints and available conventions.

## Construct or assess the result

Produce the construction, transformation, or assessment requested by the task
using the selected distinctions and expressions. Preserve the wording,
identifiers, and forms fixed by the task or applicable authority.

For a transformation, determine what must remain true or distinguishable and
which changes in meaning or behavior are authorized. Compare the source basis
and proposed result for added, removed, combined, separated, or changed
distinctions. Evaluate the change through the conclusions and actions it permits.
Establish byte identity or reversibility where the task requires those properties.

Express uncertainty and permitted choices according to the supporting information.
Resolve differences between interpretations where the task requires deciding
between their consequences. Preserve remaining open choices within their stated
conditions.

## Examine interpretation and preservation

For each applicable requirement, recover the answer, decision, or behavior from
the current result using the information available to its intended consumer.
Resolve references and definitions through the conventions and sources available
to that consumer. Identify competing readings that those conventions and that
information permit and that change a required answer, decision, or behavior.
Compare each recovered outcome with what the task and supporting information
establish. Identify the relevant part of the result, examination method,
supporting evidence, and finding.

Select cases that distinguish interpretations with different required outcomes.
Include possibilities the supporting information leaves open and outcomes the
requirements exclude. Compare what the result permits in those cases with what
the task permits or requires. Trace added certainty, restrictions, or choices
to their evidence or authority.

Use the actual interpretation and validation methods of the relevant language,
discipline, or tool. Establish required execution from observations of performed
actions. Distinguish faithful preservation of an assertion from establishing
its truth, and retained information from information its intended consumer
can recover.

Compare the requirements and applicable procedural actions with the completed
examinations. If that comparison establishes an omitted check, unsupported
finding, or discrepancy, identify the requirement and information that expose it.

## Correct discrepancies and determine completion

For an established discrepancy, locate the responsible information, expression,
relationship, interpretation, or assessment criterion. Return to that decision
and correct it within the task's authority. In an assessment-only task, present
the supported correction as a proposal.

Re-examine the changed result and the meanings or uses affected by the change.
A changed requirement or supporting fact also requires reconsidering the
decisions and examinations that depend on it. Continue correction where the
new examination establishes a remaining discrepancy.

Determine whether each unresolved point is a permitted uncertainty or choice,
or leaves required work unfinished. Obtain the information or authorization
needed for unfinished work where the task permits. Otherwise identify what
remains required and what prevents establishing it.

Deliver the requested result as complete when examination of that result
establishes satisfaction of each applicable requirement and required procedural
action. Base the completion judgment on those findings and their evidence.
